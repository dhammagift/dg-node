On click Dictionary Settings

When using a desktop device or a mouse, in addition to clicking on individual words, you can also select phrases and sentences.

To choose Dictionary style that suits your needs:

  1. Open Dhamma.gift Read.
  2. Click on the gear icon ⚙️ (Settings).
  3. Click the dropdown list "Dictionary on click" and select the desired option.
  4. Click "Apply" to save the settings.

DPD Built-in: A powerful built-in version of Digital Pali Dictionary (DPD), that provides essential features: word meanings, compound breakdowns, and basic grammar information. All words words pali and translation are clickable. New Window with Full Online DPD version will appear.

DPD Online: The full version of the DPD dictionary, offering extensive grammatical details, root information, examples, and more. With text input for futher investigation, word lookup history and settings.

DPD Online New Window: Same as above but in new browser window. Very convenient for multi-display setups.

DPD Online mini: A compact version of the full DPD dictionary with essential details. It lacks the ability to click words within the word popup, text input, word lookup history, and settings.

Search Only: This option allows you to initiate a search for the clicked word on Dhamma.gift instantly.

DictTango Android: Directly opens the DictTango Android app, enabling instant lookup in any installed dictionaries, including DPD, without copying and pasting or using the context menu + Magnifing Glass button initiates search at Dhma.gift when double clicked.



Mdict IOS: Same as above, but for Mdict iOS. Directly opens the Mdict IOS app.



GoldenDict-NG Desktop: Same as above, but for GoldenDict-NG Desktop app. Directly opens the GoldenDict-NG app.



Online versions rely on an internet connection, so they might work slower than the built-in one, but the additional features make it worth it.

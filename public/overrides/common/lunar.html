Key Suttas on Uposatha to Start with

  • an10.46 — skipping uposathas is not allowed ("it is your loss and failure").
  • mn83 — one should observe the 14th, 15th, and 8th days.
  • mn146 — 14th waxing moon, 15th full moon.
  • an3.37 — comparing the 14th, 15th, and 8th days with visits by deities of different ranks.
  • an3.70 — "nights and days" rather than "days and nights"; the Pali has the fixed expression ahoratta (“day-night”) — in other contexts.
    This sutta also explains how Uposatha should NOT be observed—the “cowherd’s Uposatha” and the “Nigaṇṭha’s Uposatha”—and how the Noble One’s Uposatha should be observed.

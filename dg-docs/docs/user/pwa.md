# PWA: install straight from the browser

<div style={{margin: '1rem 0'}}>
  <SiteLink to="/"><img src="/assets/img/buttons/pwa-cta.png" alt="Install as a PWA" style={{maxWidth: '220px'}} /></SiteLink>
</div>

In short, it's the same thing as [Dhamma.Gift online](/dhamma-gift-online)
— the same site as a standalone app with its own icon, just without
installing it through Google Play: one button right in your browser, and
an icon appears on your phone or computer, just like a regular app.

Just like Dhamma.Gift online, two small conveniences are added on top of
the site:

## Shortcuts

A long-press on the app icon on your home screen (or a right-click on
the icon in the taskbar on a computer) opens a menu of quick links —
straight to where you need, skipping the home page: table of contents,
dictionary, Patimokkha, and so on.

## "Share to Search"

You're reading anything — an article, a book in another app, a
message — you come across a word or phrase in Pali, select it and send
it to Dhamma.Gift through the regular system "Share" menu. The app opens
right away with search results for that phrase.

<AppFrame src="/?q=kacchapa&source=pwa" title="Example: search results after 'Share'" height={420} />

Both conveniences are available on Android and on a computer (Windows/
Mac). On iPhone and iPad (Safari), "Share to Search" doesn't work yet —
for a similar result there, the [browser extension](/browser-extension)
or the [Telegram bot](/telegram-bot) work better.

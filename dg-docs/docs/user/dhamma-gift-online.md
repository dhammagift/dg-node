# Dhamma.Gift online

<div style={{margin: '1rem 0'}}>
  <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=gift.dhamma.twa"><img src="/assets/img/buttons/google-play-cta.png" alt="Dhamma.Gift online on Google Play" style={{maxWidth: '220px'}} /></a>
</div>

The Android app on Google Play. It's literally the whole site (the same
search page and reader, nothing trimmed down), just in its own window,
plus two system-level perks — quick-access shortcuts and "Share to
Search" (select a word in any app → Share → Dhamma.Gift → search results
ready). How those two perks work — on a separate page,
["PWA: install straight from the browser"](/pwa).

A second, fully offline app is coming — [Dhamma.Gift Full](/dhamma-gift-full)
(still in development).

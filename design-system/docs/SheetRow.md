# SheetRow

One entry inside a Sheet — a link with an optional description, or a compact chip.

```jsx
<SheetRow label="MN 10" description="Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta" />
<SheetRow label="Gemini" chip />
```

The star sits at the leading edge, in the ordinary glyph's place: pushed to the end of the row it reads as a separate button rather than a mark on the item. Chips are for short self-evident labels.
